# ShoeMatch First 20 Users Test Plan

## Objective
Determine whether people naturally understand and repeatedly use ShoeMatch before adding AI or shopping.

## What testers should do

1. Open ShoeMatch.
2. Add at least 3 pairs of shoes.
3. Match one real outfit.
4. Review the recommendation.
5. Use Good Pick / Not for Me.
6. Try another outfit later.
7. Give short feedback.

## What we want to learn

- Can users add shoes without help?
- Is adding shoe metadata annoying?
- Do users understand that images are not automatically analyzed?
- Do recommendations feel reasonable?
- Do users trust the explanation?
- Do users use alternatives?
- Do users click Shop a better match?
- Do users come back a second time?

## Success metrics

- Activation: at least 12 users add 3+ shoes.
- Core usage: at least 12 users complete one match.
- Recommendation usefulness: at least 60% of explicit feedback is Good Pick.
- Retention: at least 5 users return and complete another match.
- Shopping interest: track Shop a better match clicks.
- Qualitative: identify the top 3 repeated complaints or requests.

## User interview script

1. What did you think ShoeMatch did before you started?
2. Was adding your shoes easy or annoying?
3. Was anything confusing?
4. Did the recommendation make sense?
5. Did you trust the reason it gave you?
6. Would you use this before work, a date, party, or normal day out?
7. Would automatic outfit detection make this more useful?
8. Would you want ShoeMatch to recommend shoes to buy?
9. Would you use this again next week?
10. What is the one thing you would change?

Ask about specific behavior and problems, not whether testers simply like the app.
